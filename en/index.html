Farming in Siyazan for
Generations

The fertile soil and clean pastures of the Siyazan district, at the foot of the Caspian Sea, create ideal conditions for raising livestock. Our family has raised cows, sheep and goats on this land for generations, letting our animals graze freely on natural feed. No synthetic hormones or antibiotics are used in our products. Our milk, cheese, meat and wool are all prepared using traditional methods at every step. That's why every bag we deliver carries the flavor of hard work preserved across generations.

Why Choose Us?

From farm to table, we stay committed to quality and naturalness at every step.

Natural Feed

Our animals graze on open pasture and are raised on natural feed, free of hormones and antibiotics.

Fresh Delivery

High Quality
