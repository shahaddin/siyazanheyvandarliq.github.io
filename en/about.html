From Siyazan to Your
Table

Siyəzən Heyvandarlıq is a family farm operating in the Siyazan district on the Caspian coast. We've raised cows, sheep and goats for years; our animals grow up on open pasture, on natural feed. Our milk, cheese, meat and wool products are prepared using traditional methods, without any artificial additives. Our goal is simple: to bring what we get from Siyazan's clean natural surroundings to your table, anywhere in Azerbaijan, by the shortest path possible.

Our Values

The principles we hold to on the way from farm to your table.

Naturalness

Our animals are never given hormones or antibiotics. They eat natural pasture and feed.

Transparency

Feel free to ask where a product comes from. Everything is sourced from our farm in Siyazan.

Tradition

We prepare products like cheese and motal using methods passed down through generations.

Reliability

We stay in touch with you from the day you order until the day it's delivered.
